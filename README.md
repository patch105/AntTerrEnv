# AntTerrEnv

## AntTerrEnv: 1 km rasters for modelling biodiversity and human impact in terrestrial Antarctica

## Project overview

Antarctica’s unique terrestrial ecosystems face growing anthropogenic threats. To safeguard Antarctica’s landscapes, we must rapidly expand our knowledge of biodiversity across the vast continent. Data-driven approaches for describing and conserving Antarctic terrestrial biodiversity through modelling could rapidly accelerate our progress. However, the availability and accessibility of high-resolution climate, topography, and human impact spatial layers relevant to terrestrial Antarctica is a critical barrier to widespread, consistent implementation of ecological modelling on the continent. This project aims to capitalise on recent advances in biodiversity data assimilation by developing a unique and timely resource, AntTerrEnv, a dataset of continent-wide, high-resolution environmental layers for modelling biodiversity and human impact in terrestrial Antarctica.

This work was supported by the Australian Research Council (ARC) SRIEAS Grant SR200100005 Securing Antarctica's Environmental Future.
